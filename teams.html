Team analysis

Every card from one team across all tracked sets. Relocated or renamed clubs are grouped under the current franchise (e.g. Oakland Raiders → Las Vegas Raiders), and the printed name is kept on each card.
Relocated & retired teams. Cards are grouped under the current franchise, and each card keeps its printed team name. MLB: Brooklyn Dodgers → LA Dodgers; New York Giants → SF Giants; Milwaukee Braves → Atlanta Braves; St. Louis Browns → Baltimore Orioles; Montreal Expos → Washington Nationals; Florida Marlins → Miami Marlins; Tampa Bay Devil Rays → Rays; Cleveland Indians → Guardians; California / Anaheim Angels → LA Angels; Oakland Athletics → Athletics. NFL: Houston Oilers → Tennessee Titans; Oakland / LA Raiders → Las Vegas Raiders; San Diego Chargers → LA Chargers; St. Louis Rams → LA Rams; Chicago / St. Louis / Phoenix Cardinals → Arizona Cardinals; Washington Redskins / Football Team → Commanders; Boston Patriots → New England Patriots; Baltimore Colts → Indianapolis Colts. NBA: New Jersey Nets → Brooklyn Nets; Seattle SuperSonics → OKC Thunder. NHL: Utah Hockey Club → Utah Mammoth. Washington Senators: the 1901–60 club became the Twins and the 1961–71 club became the Rangers. Cards don't show a year, so we only map a player whose Senators era is clear (Walter Johnson, 1907–27 → Twins); any other Senators card is listed as its own team, “Washington Senators (historic)”.
