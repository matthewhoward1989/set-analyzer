Definitions

Rarity tiers use the published hobby pack odds of an insert or parallel run. Per case = packs per case ÷ odds, and X = 1 ÷ per case (cases per pull).
SHORT PRINT 1 < X ≤ 5 (rarer than 1 per case, up to 1 per 5 cases) · SSP 5 < X ≤ 25 · GRAIL X > 25 · no tier at 1+ per case.
Example: 2026 Bowman Chrome Hobby has 6 packs × 12 boxes = 72 packs per case. A 1:288 insert is 72 ÷ 288 = 0.25 per case, about 1 per 4 cases, so Short Print. A 1:960 parallel is about 1 per 13 cases, so SSP. A 1:2,006 auto is about 1 per 28 cases, so Grail.
Odds apply to the whole run (any card in it), not to one named card. odds ? means no published odds were found, and we don't guess. named CH is a manufacturer's or breaker's "case hit" label, shown only where explicitly published and not used in metrics.
